KDRAMAS

(the best kdramas as of 2026 based on my opinion)

I'll include my realization fro each film so you'll understand why is it the best

  • "We are all Trying Here" - One thing that really stands out in this drama is how it reminds us that everyone is fighting their own battles quietly. The story doesn't romanticize life, it shows how messy, unfair, and exhausting adulthood can be, but it also proves that people keep moving forward anyway.
  • Can This Love Be Translated? - It's about being understood and choosing to understand someone else. The line about there being over 7,100 languages on earth hits hard, because its not really about spoken language. It's about how each of us expresses ourselves differently. Through actions, silence, humor, distance, and fear. We all have our own way of communicating, and that's what makes us unique.
  • A Love Other than Yours - This drama reminded us even a 10 year relationship isn't a lifetime obligation. When love becomes draining and painful, you're allowed to let go, choose yourself, and start over. You can cherish the past without being trapped in it. Namgung's two years away were about healing, rediscovering himself, and finding the courage to finally let go of emotional ties and obligations. He closed the chapter on the family he had with Mido, established boundaries, and choose personal growth. Only then could he fully embrace his feelings for Sua without carrying the weight of his past. You dont owe anyone forever just because you shared a decade. Sometimes, letting go is how you finally choose yourself and make room for a healthier love.
  • Once We Were Us(even though this film is in 2025 i'll still include it because it releases in dec 31, 2025 and its also peak) - Sometimes, letting go is the only way for us to grow. Letting go doesn't mean completely regretting and forgetting a person. Those what if's that we hold on too tight might be more painful than asking what went wrong. Closures don't always have to be bitter, they can be reflective and allow for acceptance to fully move one from that season.
